MYTH BUSTED

Myth: It's just vegetables, so you can eat the whole jar.

Reality: zacuscă is mostly peppers, eggplant and onion, which is great: fibre, vitamins, real food, made at home. But traditional recipes use a lot of sunflower oil, and one tablespoon of oil has about 120 calories. So a jar is closer to a meal than a snack. Enjoy it on bread, in normal portions, and keep the rest for winter. That's why Mama made 27 jars.
