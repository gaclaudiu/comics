MYTH BUSTED

Myth: The harder you go every single day, the faster you get fit.

Reality: CrossFit isn't the problem. Going from couch to maximum effort every day is. Muscles get stronger while they recover, not while they're being flattened. Scale the workout, learn the technique first, and take rest days. Your joints will send a thank-you card.
