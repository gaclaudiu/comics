MYTH BUSTED

Myth: Muscles grow in the gym.

Reality: the gym only sends the signal. Repair and growth happen while you recover, and deep sleep is when the body releases most of its growth hormone. In one study, a single sleepless night cut muscle-building by 18%. Short sleep also raises hunger hormones, which is why Blob loves 2 a.m. Adults need 7 hours or more.

Source: Lamon et al., Physiological Reports, 2021
