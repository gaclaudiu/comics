MYTH BUSTED

Myth: If a woman lifts heavy, she gets bulky like a bodybuilder.

Reality: women have roughly 10–20 times less testosterone than men, so building a lot of muscle takes years of focused training and eating for it. It's a goal some women choose, not something that happens by accident. What strength training does bring: stronger bones (one of the best defences against osteoporosis), better posture, easier everyday life and more confidence. And, apparently, the ability to re-hang a door before your first coffee. Bonus: being strong doesn't mean being reckless. Coach Ana is stronger than Dan and still waits for the key.
