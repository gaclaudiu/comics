MYTH BUSTED

Myth: No days off. Rest is for the weak.

Reality: training breaks the body down. It gets stronger while it recovers. Push hard for weeks without a break and you get stalled progress, niggling injuries, bad sleep, a short temper and a motivation crash. That's why many training plans include an easier “deload” week every 4 to 8 weeks: lighter weights, fewer sets, more sleep, gentle movement. Your head works the same way, and gym stress adds to work and life stress. If exhaustion, low mood or anxiety stick around even after rest, talk to a doctor or a mental health professional. That's training too.
