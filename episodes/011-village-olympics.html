MYTH BUSTED

Myth: You need a gym to get fit. And sweating a lot means you're burning fat.

Reality: long before gyms, people got strong by working: digging, carrying, lifting and walking all day. Gyms just package those movements, and the farmer's carry is literally named after it. Gardening, stairs and carrying shopping all count. Build up gradually, bend your knees and keep your back straight: Bunica has 60 years of practice. And sweat is mostly water you'll drink back. Fat loss comes from regular movement and what you eat over weeks, not from one sweaty afternoon.
