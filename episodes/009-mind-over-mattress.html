Lesson two: how a habit is built.

Six Mondays later. Still raining. Still 6:00 a.m.

DANNot motivated. Going anyway. Just ten minutes.

THE BRAINThat's it. That's the whole secret. Nobody believes it's this boring.

BLOBFine. But my 10 p.m. snack habit has a 400-day streak. Respect the streak.

MYTH BUSTED

Myth: People who stick with training are just more motivated than you.

Reality: everyone's motivation goes up and down, pros included. What keeps them going is routine: a fixed time, gear ready the night before, a small goal for bad days, and a way to track progress. Habits form by repeating the same thing in the same context. In one well-known study it took about two months on average, with big differences between people, and missing a single day didn't break the habit. Blob's snack habit works exactly the same way. Use his weapon against him.
