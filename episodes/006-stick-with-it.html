MYTH BUSTED

Myth: Colourful kinesio tape protects your knees from injury.

Reality: the evidence that tape prevents injuries is weak. Some people feel a bit less pain or more confident with it, and that's about it. What actually protects knees: strong legs, good technique and not doubling your workload overnight. To take tape off, soak it with oil or in the shower, hold the skin down, and peel slowly in the direction the hair grows.
