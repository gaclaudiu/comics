MYTH BUSTED

Myth: When your mind says “I'm done,” your body is completely empty.

Reality: usually not. Your brain calls “stop” early to keep a safety reserve. That's the idea behind David Goggins' famous “40% rule.” The exact 40% is a motivational number, not a measurement, but the reserve is real: cyclists told they were racing their own best ride (secretly 2% more powerful) rode faster than their best. Still: sharp pain, chest pain or dizziness mean stop, not push.

Source: Stone et al., Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 2012
