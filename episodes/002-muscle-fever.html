MYTH BUSTED

Myth: Muscle fever is lactic acid stuck in your muscles, and the more it hurts, the better the workout.

Reality: lactic acid clears within about an hour. The soreness (DOMS) comes from tiny damage in the muscle fibers after new or unusually hard exercise, especially the lowering part of a move. It peaks 1 to 3 days later, fades as you train regularly, and says nothing about how good the workout was. Light movement helps. (Blob hates that part.)
