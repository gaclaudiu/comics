MYTH BUSTED

Myth: Shoulder pain? Push through it, or let a search engine diagnose you.

Reality: a common beginner cause is too much overhead work, too soon, with no warm-up. Pain that lasts more than a few days, gets worse when you lift your arm, or wakes you at night is a job for a physiotherapist or doctor, not for Flex, Blob or a search bar. Rehab is often boring bands and patience. Boring works.
