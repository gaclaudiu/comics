MYTH BUSTED

Myth: Train like a pro and you'll look like a pro.

Reality: pros like Seba build their workload over years, with coaches, physios, a nutrition plan and a lot of sleep. Copying their sessions as a beginner mostly gets you injured. Copy their habits instead: warm up properly, train consistently, eat real meals, sleep 7 to 9 hours, and recover like it's part of the job. Because for them, it is.
