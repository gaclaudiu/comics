MYTH BUSTED

Myth: A 10-minute workout is an easy workout.

Reality: how hard a workout is depends on intensity, not on the clock. A metcon is meant to be done close to your limit, which is why ten minutes can feel like an hour. It's also why short workouts work: in one study, 10-minute sessions with just three 20-second all-out sprints improved fitness about as much as 50 minutes of steady cycling. Short isn't easy. Short is efficient.

Source: Gillen et al., PLoS ONE, 2016
