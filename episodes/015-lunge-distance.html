MYTH BUSTED

Myth: If an exercise leaves you limping, skip it. Stick to the ones that feel easy.

Reality: lunges feel brutal because they're hard: one leg does the work while also keeping you balanced, so they find every weak spot and every left-right difference. That's exactly why they're worth doing. And there's good news, called the repeated bout effect: after the first tough session your muscles adapt, and the same workout leaves you far less sore the next time. Start small (bodyweight, shorter steps; reverse lunges are kinder to the knees), do them once or twice a week, and add a little at a time. Muscle soreness is normal. Sharp pain in a joint is not: that's a signal to stop and get it checked.
