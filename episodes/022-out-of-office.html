MYTH BUSTED

Myth: You need hours of free time to get fit.

Reality: the WHO recommends 150–300 minutes of moderate activity a week (or 75–150 vigorous), plus strength training on 2 or more days. That's roughly 20–45 minutes a day. And short bouts count: the 2020 guidelines dropped the old “at least 10 minutes at a time” rule. A brisk walk at lunch, stairs instead of the lift, squats between calls. No outage required.

Source: Bull et al., WHO 2020 guidelines, British Journal of Sports Medicine
