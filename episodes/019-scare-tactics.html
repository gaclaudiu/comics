MYTH BUSTED

Myth: You have to keep shocking or “confusing” your muscles, or they stop growing.

Reality: muscles don't get scared or confused. They grow from progressive overload: doing a little more over time, consistently. Swapping some exercises now and then is fine and keeps training fun, but changing everything all the time makes progress hard to track and can slow it down. Same plan, a bit heavier each week. And kettlebells only work if you lift them.

Source: Kassiano et al., Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research, 2022
