MYTH BUSTED

Myth: A glass of red wine a day is good for your heart.

Reality: the idea came from old studies where many “non-drinkers” had quit because they were already ill, which made moderate drinkers look healthier. Better research finds no real benefit, and the WHO says no amount of alcohol is good for your health. A glass of wine has about 120 calories, slows fat burning while your liver clears the alcohol, and makes your sleep worse. The fresh must? A mug has about as much sugar as a can of cola. Drink wine for the taste, not as medicine. The healthy part of Dan's day was the harvest. Safety tip: fermenting wine fills the cellar with carbon dioxide, so air it out before you go down.
