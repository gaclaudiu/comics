MYTH BUSTED

Myth: A walking pad melts the fat while you work. Who needs a gym?

Reality: slow walking at a desk burns roughly 90–120 extra calories an hour compared with sitting. That's real, and moving instead of sitting all day is good for you. But one croissant cancels about an hour of it, and strolling won't build strength. Treat it as a bonus on top of your workouts, not a replacement. And maybe sit down for the big presentation.

Source: Koepp, Moore & Levine, Frontiers in Physiology, 2017
