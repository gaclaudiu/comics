MYTH BUSTED

Myth: 10,000 steps a day is the scientific magic number.

Reality: it started as the name of a Japanese pedometer, the “manpo-kei” (10,000-step meter), sold after the 1964 Tokyo Olympics. A 2022 review of 15 studies found the benefit levels off around 6,000–8,000 steps for people over 60 and 8,000–10,000 for younger adults. Every walk counts. The last 600 steps at midnight, instead of sleep, don't.

Source: ScienceAlert, on the 2022 Lancet Public Health review
