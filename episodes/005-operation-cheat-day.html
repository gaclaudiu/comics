MYTH BUSTED

Myth: Eating clean means zero treats forever, and one slice of cake undoes weeks of work.

Reality: progress comes from what you do most of the time, not from one slice. Strict all-or-nothing rules often backfire into a full pizza weekend. And yes, short sleep and stress really do make cravings louder. Blob didn't invent that. He just uses it.
