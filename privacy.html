Short version: this is a webcomic. If you click “Sure, have one” on the cookie banner, Google Analytics counts your visit so we can see how many people read each episode. If you click “No thanks,” nothing is tracked. There are no ads, no accounts, and we never sell data.

Who runs this site

Flex vs Blob (flexandblob.com) is a personal, non-commercial webcomic.

What we collect, and only if you say yes

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the comic is read. It tells us things like:

  • which pages and episodes were viewed, and for roughly how long
  • your approximate location (country or city, never your exact address)
  • your device type, browser and screen size
  • how you found the site (for example a search engine or a social media link)

We see this only as totals and trends. We can't see who you are, and we don't try to find out. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses.

If you click “No thanks,” Google Analytics is never loaded and none of this is collected.

Other services the site uses

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser loads the site, and may keep it in security logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The comic's lettering fonts load from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. This happens for every visitor, before any cookie choice.

Why we're allowed to do this

Analytics runs only with your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Hosting and fonts are needed to show you the site at all, which is our legitimate interest in running it (Article 6(1)(f)).

Where the data goes

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States. Both take part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the EU recognises as offering adequate protection.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your mind at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page. Saying no later also deletes the Google Analytics cookies.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask what data exists about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Since we can't identify individual readers, there is usually nothing tied to you personally, but you can always ask.
  • If you think something is wrong, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or the authority in your own country.

What we never do

  • Show ads or use advertising cookies
  • Sell or share data with anyone for marketing
  • Ask you to create an account or give us your email
